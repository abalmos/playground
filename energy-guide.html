The Seven Main Chakras

A complete guide to understanding and working with the body's energy centers.

Crown Chakra (Sahasrara)

Location: Top of head

Element: Thought/Cosmic Energy

Color: Violet/White

Purpose: Spiritual connection, enlightenment, divine consciousness

Balanced: Spiritual awareness, inner peace, wisdom

Imbalanced: Disconnection, confusion, lack of purpose

Healing: Meditation, prayer, silence, connection to nature

Third Eye Chakra (Ajna)

Location: Forehead, between eyebrows

Element: Light

Color: Indigo

Purpose: Intuition, imagination, wisdom, decision making

Balanced: Clear thinking, strong intuition, insight

Imbalanced: Confusion, poor judgment, lack of focus

Healing: Visualization, dream work, star gazing

Throat Chakra (Vishuddha)

Location: Throat

Element: Ether/Sound

Color: Blue

Purpose: Communication, self-expression, truth

Balanced: Clear communication, authentic expression

Imbalanced: Difficulty speaking truth, fear of judgment

Healing: Singing, chanting, journaling, speaking affirmations

Heart Chakra (Anahata)

Location: Center of chest

Element: Air

Color: Green/Pink

Purpose: Love, compassion, relationships, emotional balance

Balanced: Compassion, empathy, forgiveness, love

Imbalanced: Jealousy, fear of intimacy, loneliness

Healing: Acts of kindness, gratitude practice, heart-opening yoga

Solar Plexus Chakra (Manipura)

Location: Upper abdomen

Element: Fire

Color: Yellow

Purpose: Personal power, confidence, self-esteem, willpower

Balanced: Confidence, self-respect, strong will

Imbalanced: Low self-esteem, control issues, anger

Healing: Core strengthening, sun exposure, positive affirmations

Sacral Chakra (Svadhisthana)

Location: Lower abdomen

Element: Water

Color: Orange

Purpose: Creativity, sexuality, emotions, pleasure

Balanced: Creativity, emotional stability, healthy sexuality

Imbalanced: Emotional instability, creative blocks, addiction

Healing: Creative expression, water activities, hip-opening exercises

Root Chakra (Muladhara)

Location: Base of spine

Element: Earth

Color: Red

Purpose: Grounding, security, survival, stability

Balanced: Feeling grounded, secure, stable

Imbalanced: Anxiety, fear, financial stress

Healing: Walking barefoot, grounding exercises, physical activity

Reiki Symbols

Cho Ku Rei - The Power Symbol

Used to increase or decrease power. Can be used at the beginning of a session to boost energy.

Sei He Ki - The Mental/Emotional Symbol

Used for emotional and mental healing, clearing negative patterns.

Hon Sha Ze Sho Nen - The Distance Symbol

Used for distance healing, connecting across time and space.
